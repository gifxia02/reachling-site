How we work

A disciplined process, not a one-size-fits-all playbook

For live-event campaigns, we use a seven-phase operating rhythm from announcement through showtime. The sequence is consistent; the audiences, creative, spend, and decisions are built around each campaign.

01

Announce

Launch creative, seed awareness, set up pixel and event signals, build audience pools.

02

On-Sale

High-intent conversion push with buyer signals, creative split tests and market segmentation.

03

Learn

Read CPA, CTR, conversion quality, frequency, pacing and inventory signals.

04

Scale

Shift spend toward winners, expand or contract audiences, refresh creative.

05

Retarget

Re-engage site visitors, engagers and high-intent audiences with the right message.

06

Final Push

Adjust urgency, budget and creative based on time-to-show and remaining inventory.

07

Retain

Capture audience learnings for the next show instead of starting from zero.
